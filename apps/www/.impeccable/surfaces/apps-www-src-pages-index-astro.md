# Laman marketing site: home

Scope: the home page of the Laman marketing site (`apps/www`), BM at `/` and English at `/en`. Mode: Persuade.

Audience: federal ministries and departments, state governments, and local authorities (PBT); the heads of ICT and corporate communications who choose, and the officer who will run the site. Job: decide Laman beats WordPress, Joomla or Drupal for their agency. Action: Pasang Laman (install guide, GitHub) or Hubungi pasukan. Proof: Laman's real checks and screens, a built-in-versus-plugin comparison that's sourced and dated, real MCP tool names, measured page weight. Positioning (owner, 4 Nov 2026): lead with how complete Laman is out of the box and that it's built for AI agents; sovereignty is a supporting point lower on the page. Write with confidence, never by putting other tools down. Constraints: FTDS tokens and components, Inter, the primary field allowed on this surface. No "AI-native" claim: Laman has no AI inside the admin; it is built for AI agents over MCP. Say plainly that content an officer gives an agent goes to the AI provider the agency chooses.

Memorable moment: the Sunrise, an abstract field of FTDS colours that flows behind a short hopeful line, with gold light rising along its contours.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Open on feeling, then prove it: a bold, abstract, hopeful first viewport with almost no words, then everything that makes Laman the complete choice (who it's for, built in versus plugins, AI agents, the steps, what visitors get, sovereignty). It refuses the category's headline-over-screenshot hero and the feature-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: The Sunrise (one WebGL shader over FTDS Primary, Success, Danger and Warning: blue, a little teal, and a coral-to-gold sunrise from below, with fine contour lines) full-bleed under the white navbar. Below, FTDS on white and Paper sections; the Primary 700 field returns for the AI-agent section and the closing band, and the Primary 950 stage holds the boundary diagram.

STORY: Visitors are met by the Sunrise and one line ("Laman web untuk setiap rakyat."); read who Laman is for (ministries, states, PBT); see that every need is built in where other tools need plugins; see an AI agent do a real task safely; then the steps, what visitors get, and that it all stays on their servers; and act on Pasang Laman.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The Sunrise fills it (full height less the navbar). Centred: the H1 in white (up to 104px, at most 14 characters a line), a one-line lead, and two field buttons (Pasang Laman, Lihat apa yang terbina dalam). Same on phones, stacked.

FORM: Abstract art and simple text, the owner's call on 4 Nov 2026 after the poster-to-page stage, an animated poster prototype and a letters-to-page prototype were tried and dropped. Signature interaction: the field leans toward the pointer. Motion grammar: the Sunrise flows slowly and continuously while on screen; reduced motion draws one still frame; no WebGL leaves a CSS gradient.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
